# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in the RedDotRelay community a harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible
disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression,
level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our standards

Positive behavior includes empathy, respectful disagreement, constructive
feedback, accepting responsibility, and focusing on what is best for the
community. Unacceptable behavior includes sexualized language or attention,
trolling, insults, harassment, publishing another person's private information,
and conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate professionally.

## Enforcement responsibilities

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing acceptable behavior.
They may remove, edit, or reject contributions and may temporarily or
permanently restrict participation for conduct they deem inappropriate,
threatening, offensive, or harmful.

## Scope and reporting

This code applies in project spaces and when representing the project publicly.
Report conduct incidents privately to the maintainer contact published in
`MAINTAINERS.md`. Do not use public issues for sensitive reports. Security
vulnerabilities follow `SECURITY.md` instead.

Maintainers will respect the reporter's privacy and apply consequences
proportionate to impact and pattern: correction, warning, temporary restriction,
or permanent removal. A maintainer with a conflict of interest must recuse.
